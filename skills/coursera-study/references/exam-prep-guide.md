# exam-prep.md — what to remember to pass the tests

The learner skips the videos and relies on this note to pass the graded **Module Challenge**. Its job is to predict what the challenge will test and make that unforgettable. Quiz contents are never available, so infer them from signals.

## 1. Find the exam signals (strongest first)
1. **Module description** (what the learner "will learn to …") — each verb phrase is effectively a learning objective. Turn it into 3–6 objectives.
2. **Glossary** readings and terms the course defines explicitly ("X is …", "we call this …").
3. **Introduction / Wrap-up** videos — the instructor states what matters.
4. **Emphasis** in transcripts: "key", "important", "remember", "the main …", numbered lists ("three steps", "five phases"), contrasts ("X, not Y").
5. **The course case study** (e.g. Office Green / Plant Pals in the Google PM certificate) — scenario questions reuse it.
6. Practice "Test your knowledge" items and their lesson scope (from the test map).
7. `my/mistakes.md` if the learner wrote one — their real weak spots.

Score each concept: ⭐⭐⭐ appears in 3+ signal types, ⭐⭐ in 2, ⭐ in 1.

## Rules
- Definitions stay in the course's English wording — the test uses those exact words. Vietnamese only explains.
- Every Must-know item cites its source paragraph with a full-path block link.
- Do not invent facts beyond the sources; anything added from general PM knowledge is marked *(bổ sung)*.
- Every Must-know item gets at least one flashcard.
