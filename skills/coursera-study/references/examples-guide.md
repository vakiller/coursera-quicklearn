# examples.md — real-world examples that make the idea click

The learner is a working adult with little time. A good example lets them grasp *why the concept exists* in under a minute and remember it at exam time. Generic examples ("in some project…") do not do that.

## Personalise with `Coursera/_profile.md`
Read it first. Use the learner's role, industry and current projects for the **work example**. If it is missing or empty, use a believable software/operations project and say once at the top that filling in `_profile.md` will make examples sharper.

## One block per key concept (⭐⭐⭐ first, then ⭐⭐)
```markdown
## <Concept>

> [!tip] Bản chất
> <one sentence: what problem this concept solves>

> [!example] Đời thường
> <familiar analogy — wedding, house renovation, trip — 2–4 sentences>

> [!example] Trong công việc của bạn
> <scenario from the learner's context with named roles, numbers, a date, the decision taken and its consequence>

> [!quote] Trong khóa học
> <how the course illustrates it, e.g. the Office Green case> — [[…#^t0102|nguồn]]

> [!warning] Làm sai thì sao
> <counter-example: skip the concept → what breaks, concretely>

> [!question]- Trong đề sẽ hỏi kiểu
> <one scenario question in the course style>
> **Đáp án:** … — <why>
```

## Quality bar
- Concrete: people (roles), numbers, a decision and its consequence. ≤ 120 words per callout.
- The work example must be plausible for the learner's industry, not a copy of the course case.
- Invented examples are illustrations, not course facts — never cite a source for them; only "Trong khóa học" cites.
- When the concept already has `Coursera/_concepts/<Concept>.md`, append the best work example there under `## Ví dụ` instead of creating a second concept note.
